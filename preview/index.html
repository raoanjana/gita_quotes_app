What's happening, and what it means

Add to Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit (or +) in the top corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for “Gita Quotes”, choose a size, and tap Add Widget.
  4. A new verse arrives every morning at 4 am. Tap the widget to open it here.
  5. On medium and large widgets, tap “Explain this to me” for the backstory and meaning.
  6. Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap the area below the clock, and pick Gita Quotes. It shows the same verse as your Home Screen; long verses appear as a short excerpt.
